# Mark an inference as an inference

Write what a source says as a quote or a cited fact. Write what you conclude as your inference, and say so. Never write an inference as a quote or as a decision.

Reason: readers act on decisions and quotes. A guess that reads as a decision becomes one.

Example: "The customer asked for SSO (call notes, 10-21). I think they will need SCIM too; they did not say so."
